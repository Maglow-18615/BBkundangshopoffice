8你心里大概在想What you're probably thinking

问得好。Fair questions.

Kundang 会不会太偏？Isn't Kundang too far out?

离 LATAR 收费站 2 公里，约 25 分钟到 Kepong 和 Kota Damansara。更重要的是，店铺做的是本地 7–8 万人的生意，不是靠外地人开车进来。最好的判断方法，是傍晚自己到 Jalan Kundang 站十分钟看车流。It's 2 km from the LATAR toll and about 25 minutes from Kepong and Kota Damansara. More importantly, these shops serve the 70–80K people already here. The best test: stand on Jalan Kundang for ten minutes at dusk and watch the traffic.

还没建好，现在买会不会太早？It isn't built yet — is it too early?

预计 2028 年底完工。期房的好处是现在可以选位置——转角、面向大路、双门面这些单位是先被选走的，上面的单位图你已经看到了。等建好再来，剩下的是别人挑剩的。Completion is expected end-2028. Buying early means choosing position — corner, main-road and dual-frontage units go first, as the unit board above shows. After completion you choose from what others left.

99 年地契，会不会吃亏？Is 99-year leasehold a problem?

这是商业地契的 99 年租赁地。Selangor 多数新商业项目都是这个性质，银行照常批贷款（配合银行：UOB、AmBank、CIMB）。看地时我会把地契资料给你看。It's a commercial title on 99-year leasehold, typical for new commercial projects in Selangor and financed as usual by the panel banks (UOB, AmBank, CIMB). I'll walk you through the title details at the viewing.

租不租得出去？Will it actually rent out?

没有人能保证。可以看的是事实：人口 7–8 万并在增长、大路门面、全区只有 50 间、旁边还有约 600 户的住宅项目在筹备。销售资料的目标净回报是 4.5%–5.0%，属于估算。来现场，我带你看周边现有店铺的出租情况。Nobody can guarantee it. What you can check are the facts: 70–80K and growing, main-road frontage, only 50 units, and ~600 homes planned next door. The 4.5%–5.0% target yield is an estimate. On site I'll show you how existing shops nearby are letting.
